We talked to Point users and added an onboarding

November 22, 2026

We started talking to people who use Point. Two things came up right away.

Point had no onboarding

You installed Point, it asked for some permissions, and then it sat in your menu bar. Nothing told you what to do next. So we added a welcome that opens the first time you launch it.

It starts with what Point is, and a short clip of it working:

Then the three permissions, with one line each on why Point needs them:

Then you try it. First, hold the key and talk. Your words show up in the window:

Then hold the key, talk, and circle a button. The window shows what your agent would get:

And a short list of what to remember:

The practice runs stay in the window. They don't paste into whatever app is behind it. If you already use Point, you can see the welcome from the menu bar icon, under Welcome to Point.

The default key was hard to press

Point's key used to be Right Option. People told us it was awkward to reach and hold.

So the default is now Left Control. The goal is for it to be the Fn key eventually, but a lot of other apps already use Fn. I still use Granola sometimes to compare performance, and it's one of them. For now, Left Control is working best.

If you already use Point, your key stays as it was. You can change it any time from the menu bar icon, under Trigger key.

Get the update

Both changes are in Point 0.1.3. Download it here, or see what else is in the release.

We'd love to hear any other feedback. Open an issue on GitHub and tell us what's missing or what's annoying. Thanks.

Point is a free, open-source Mac app: hold a key, talk, and circle what you mean. Your agent gets your words plus a screenshot of exactly what you circled.
