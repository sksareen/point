From a key to a wand.

Point starts as a key you hold on your Mac. Where it's going: anything you can point at, you can talk about. Here's what works today, what we're building next, and the far-out idea.

Shipped

In 0.1.2, today.

  • Hold a key and talkOn-device dictation pasted at your text cursor.
  • Circle while you talkYour words plus a screenshot with your loop on it, for Claude Code, Cursor, or any chat.
  • The picture in any chatclaude.ai, ChatGPT, Slack, and the Claude app get the screenshot itself, and it stays on your clipboard.
  • Sticky padDouble-tap for a floating note that saves as you type.
  • TimelineEvery capture, searchable, with the screenshot, transcript, and recording.

Next

The coming weeks.

  • Notarized download and HomebrewOne-click install, no warnings.
  • A faster startYour first word is never clipped.
  • Voice commands"Open Figma." Handled on your Mac.
  • Record a bug reproNarrate while you record. Your agent gets a screenshot at every click.

Later

Once the basics are solid.

  • More MacsOlder macOS and Intel, with a local Whisper or Parakeet engine as an alternative to Apple's.
  • Agents that ask you to pointYour agent asks "which button?", and you answer by circling it instead of typing.

Far out: the wand.

One day, Point leaves the keyboard. Aim a wand at any screen in the room, click to select what you're pointing at, or hold the button and draw on it. Talk while you point, same as the key.

A weighted gyroscope in the tipKeeps your aim steady, so pointing from across the room lands where you mean.
One buttonClick to select what you're pointing at. Hold it and move to draw lines on the screen.
A mic in the handleTalk while you point. The wand is just another way to trigger Point.
Stacked steel ringsA body machined in segments, like a rocket's hull. Heavy enough to feel deliberate.
